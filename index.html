What works here?

Can I apply to a real company?

No. The demo wizard creates a local tracking entry only. Official and employer websites are separate. Manual tracking does not verify employer receipt.

Does the portal contain every open job?

No. This version contains twelve examples. The intended product serves nationwide employers of every size, but no live listing feeds are connected.

Is the support widget AI?

No. It uses a small set of local keyword rules to explain navigation and limitations. It does not contact support, generate AI answers, or make network requests.

What happens after 2026?

The calendar clearly reports that no future-year dates are loaded. Timers clamp at zero, and the sandbox remains a simulation.

Your data controls

Jobs saved, preferences, drafts, scenario settings, notes, and history remain in local storage. Browser sessions and devices do not synchronize. Document filenames are session-only. Chat messages are session-only.

No external fonts, images, analytics, API credentials, or JavaScript libraries are loaded by this file. External links and a hosting provider, if used, have their own data practices.

Reset restores the five project example tracking entries. It does not affect real applications or employer accounts.
